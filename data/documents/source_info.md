# Healthcare Corpus Documentation & Metadata Registry

## Provenance Disclosure & Licensing Notice
The documents in this repository are **curated source-derived reference summaries** compiled directly from publicly accessible, evidence-based guidelines and fact sheets published by authoritative public health institutions (NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, CDC, WHO, USDA/HHS, and NLM MedlinePlus).

These documents are **not** official publications or direct distribution artifacts of the respective agencies. They are clean, structured, page-aware reference documents prepared for local RAG benchmarking and educational demonstration. All diagnostic criteria, Tolerable Upper Intake Levels, Recommended Dietary Allowances, and lifestyle recommendations are faithfully preserved from the cited source guidelines.

### 5. `nih_ods_vitamin_d.pdf` (2 Pages)
- **Title**: Vitamin D Fact Sheet for Health Professionals
- **Organization**: National Institutes of Health (NIH) - Office of Dietary Supplements
- **Official URL**: https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminD-HealthProfessional/
- **Key Topics**:
  - Intestinal calcium absorption and bone mineralization
  - Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA): 600 IU (15 mcg) for ages 1-70, 800 IU (20 mcg) for ages 71+
  - Tolerable Upper Intake Level (UL): 4,000 IU (100 mcg) daily for adults
  - Cutaneous UVB synthesis, fatty fish sources (salmon, trout), fortified milk/plant milks
  - Deficiency consequences: rickets in children, osteomalacia/osteoporosis in adults

- **Organization**: National Institutes of Health (NIH) - Office of Dietary Supplements
- **Official URL**: https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Calcium-HealthProfessional/
- **Key Topics**:
  - Structural role in bones/teeth (99%) and vascular/muscle/nerve signaling (1%)
  - Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA): 1,000 mg/day for adults 19-50, 1,200 mg for women 51+ and men 71+
